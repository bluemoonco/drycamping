Privacy

What this website stores and what happens when you use the tools.

Updated September 24, 2026 · DryCamping.com

Calculator inputs

The battery, water, and solar calculators process your inputs in your browser. This site’s calculator code does not send those inputs to a server. There are no user accounts or signup forms.

Checklist storage

If your browser permits it, the checklist saves checkmarks in local storage under the key “drycamping-checklist-v1”. This helps the list stay checked when you revisit it on the same browser. Use Reset on the checklist or clear this site’s browser data to remove that saved progress.

Hosting and connection information

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As part of delivering and protecting the site, the hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses, request details, and security-related data. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its practices.

Analytics and advertising

This version of the site does not include advertising scripts or site-installed third-party analytics. We will update this page if that changes. The hosting provider may maintain its own operational and security records.

External links

Links to land managers, manufacturers, and other resources take you to separately operated websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.
