About DryCamping.com

Independent planning tools and practical information for RV camping without hookups.

Updated September 24, 2026 · DryCamping.com

Useful information before you unplug

DryCamping.com helps RV travelers understand the basics of camping without water, sewer, or electrical hookups. The site focuses on power, water, waste capacity, preparation, and finding official campsite information.

How we approach the guides

Our articles synthesize published information and link to relevant primary sources, including land managers and equipment manufacturers. They are not presented as firsthand campground reports or hands-on product tests. We use AI-assisted research and drafting; that is not a substitute for checking a destination’s current rules or your equipment manual.

Each guide shows its update date. Location rules and conditions can change after publication, so use the linked official sources before traveling. We do not maintain a verified campsite database.

How the calculators work

The tools use transparent arithmetic and adjustable assumptions. Formulas and worked examples appear below each calculator. Defaults are illustrative examples, not guarantees or universal usage recommendations. Results depend on the accuracy of your inputs and omit some real-world effects explained on each page.

The calculators run in your browser. You do not need an account or an email address to use them. The checklist can save your checkmarks locally on the device you are using.

Images and commercial relationships

The homepage campsite image is AI-generated and illustrates the subject. It does not depict a verified or bookable camping location. At launch, this site contains no paid product placements, advertisements, or affiliate links. Any future commercial relationships will be identified where relevant.

A good place to start

Read the beginner’s guide, estimate your battery runtime and water limits, then use the checklist to prepare for a short first trip.
